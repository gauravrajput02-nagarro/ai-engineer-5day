AquaPure X200 Water Purifier - Warranty, Returns and Support

1. Warranty
The AquaPure X200 has a 2 year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in the pump, the electronics, and the tank. The filter cartridge is a consumable and is not covered by the warranty.
The warranty does not cover damage from freezing, use of non-genuine filters, use of bleach or abrasive cleaners, installation with a wrench on the adapter, or repairs by unauthorized technicians.

2. Returns and refunds
You can return the unit within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if it is in original condition with all accessories. The refund is issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned unit is received. Opened filter cartridges cannot be returned.
Return shipping is free if the unit is defective, and costs 15 USD otherwise.

3. Repairs
For warranty repairs, contact support with your order number and the error code shown. A technician visit is scheduled within 5 business days. If the unit cannot be repaired it is replaced.

4. Buying filters
Genuine FC-300 cartridges are sold in packs of 1 for 24 USD, packs of 2 for 44 USD, and packs of 4 for 80 USD. A yearly subscription of 2 cartridges costs 40 USD and ships every 6 months.
