AquaPure X200 Water Purifier - Maintenance

1. Replacing the filter
Replace the FC-300 filter cartridge every 6 months or after 3000 liters, whichever comes first. When the filter is due, the status light blinks orange and the unit beeps twice at start.
Steps: close the tap, unplug the unit, open the front door by pressing the two side tabs, twist the old cartridge a quarter turn counter-clockwise and pull it out, insert the new cartridge and twist a quarter turn clockwise until it clicks. Close the door, plug the unit in, and hold the Start button for 10 seconds to reset the filter counter. Then run the 10 minute first flush again.
Only genuine FC-300 cartridges are supported. Third-party cartridges void the warranty.

2. Cleaning the tank
Clean the internal tank every 3 months. Unplug the unit and drain the tank using the drain valve on the bottom. Wipe the inside with a soft cloth and a solution of 1 teaspoon of baking soda in 1 liter of water. Rinse twice. Never use bleach, vinegar, or abrasive cleaners because they damage the tank coating.

3. Cleaning the outside
Wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Do not spray water directly on the unit. Keep the unit away from the stove or other heat sources.

4. Storing or moving the unit
If you will not use the unit for more than 2 weeks, drain the tank and remove the filter cartridge. Store the cartridge sealed in a clean plastic bag in a cool place. Before reuse, flush for 10 minutes.

5. Frost
Never leave the unit where the temperature can fall below 0 degrees Celsius. Frozen water can crack the tank and is not covered by the warranty.
